Cold plunge and fasting are two powerful biohacking tools. Combined, they create a synergistic effect for fat burning and cellular repair.

## Why This Combo Works

### 1. Enhanced Fat Burning
Fasting depletes glycogen stores, forcing your body to burn fat. Cold plunge activates brown fat, which burns even more calories. Together:
- Maximum fat oxidation
- Accelerated ketone production
- Enhanced metabolic flexibility

### 2. Autophagy Boost
Both fasting and cold exposure trigger autophagy (cellular cleanup):
- Fasting activates AMPK pathway
- Cold exposure activates SIRT1 (NAD+-dependent)
- Combined effect may be greater than either alone

### 3. Growth Hormone
Both interventions increase growth hormone:
- Fasting: Up to 5x increase in GH
- Cold exposure: Significant GH release
- Combined: Enhanced anti-aging effects

## Protocol

### Option 1: Morning Fasted Cold Plunge
1. Wake up (don't eat)
2. Cold plunge for 3-5 minutes
3. Continue fasting for 1-2 hours after
4. Break fast with healthy meal

### Option 2: Extended Fast + Cold Plunge
1. 16-24 hour fast
2. Cold plunge during fasting window
3. Continue fasting after cold plunge
4. Break fast when ready

## Important Notes

- Start with shorter fasts (16 hours) before combining
- Stay hydrated during fasted cold plunge
- Break your fast if you feel dizzy or unwell
- Don't do extreme cold during extended fasts (24+ hours)
