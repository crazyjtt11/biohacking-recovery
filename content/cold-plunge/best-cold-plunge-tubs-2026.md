## Why Cold Plunge?

Cold water immersion has gained massive popularity thanks to researchers like Dr. Andrew Huberman and Wim Hof. The science-backed benefits include:

- **Reduced inflammation** — Cold exposure constricts blood vessels and reduces swelling
- **Faster muscle recovery** — Athletes use cold plunge to speed up post-workout recovery
- **Improved mood** — Cold exposure triggers norepinephrine release, boosting alertness and mood
- **Better sleep** — Regular cold plunge users report improved sleep quality
- **Enhanced immune function** — Studies show cold exposure may boost white blood cell count

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long should I stay in a cold plunge?

Start with 1-2 minutes and gradually work up to 5-10 minutes. Research suggests 11 minutes total per week (spread across 2-4 sessions) is optimal for most people.

### How cold should a cold plunge be?

Most experts recommend 39-55°F (4-13°C) for therapeutic benefits. Beginners should start warmer and gradually decrease temperature.

### Is cold plunge safe?

Cold plunge is generally safe for healthy adults. Consult your doctor if you have cardiovascular conditions, Raynaud's disease, or are pregnant.
