NAD+ plays a role in maintaining eye health. Here's what the research shows.

## NAD+'s Role in Eye Health

### 1. Retinal Function
The retina is one of the most metabolically active tissues. NAD+ supports:
- Photoreceptor function
- Retinal energy metabolism
- Light signal processing

### 2. Protection from Light Damage
NAD+-dependent enzymes protect against:
- UV damage
- Blue light damage
- Oxidative stress in retinal cells

### 3. Age-Related Protection
NAD+ levels decline with age in retinal cells, contributing to:
- Age-related macular degeneration
- Reduced visual acuity
- Retinal degeneration

## Research Findings

### Animal Studies
**2020 (Cell Reports):**
- NMN protected retinal cells from damage in mice
- Improved visual function
- Enhanced mitochondrial health in retinal cells

### Human Studies
**2021 (Journals of Gerontology):**
- 670nm red light (which interacts with NAD+ pathways) improved visual function
- Enhanced color contrast sensitivity
- Effects lasted up to one week

## Practical Implications

### For Eye Health Support
- NMN 500mg daily may support retinal health
- Combine with lutein and zeaxanthin
- Protect eyes from UV and blue light

### Important Notes
- Research is still early
- Human studies are limited
- NAD+ supplements are not a replacement for eye care
