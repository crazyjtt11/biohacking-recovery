NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide) has become one of the most popular anti-aging supplements. Here's everything you need to know.

## What is NMN?

NMN is a naturally occurring molecule that's a direct precursor to NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide). NAD+ is essential for:

- Cellular energy production
- DNA repair
- Sirtuin activation (longevity genes)
- Metabolic function
- Hundreds of enzymatic reactions

NMN is found in small amounts in foods like broccoli, avocado, and edamame, but supplementing provides much higher doses than diet alone.

## How NMN Works

Your body converts NMN to NAD+ through a simple one-step process:

NMN → NAD+

This makes NMN one of the most efficient ways to boost NAD+ levels, as it's only one step away from the active molecule.

NMN is generally very safe. Possible mild side effects:
- Nausea (usually at high doses)
- Headaches (add TMG to prevent)
- Insomnia (take in morning only)

No serious adverse effects have been reported in human clinical trials.
