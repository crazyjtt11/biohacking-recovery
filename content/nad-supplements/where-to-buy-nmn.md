With so many NMN sellers online, it's hard to know who to trust. Here's your guide to buying high-quality NMN.

## What to Look For

### 1. Third-Party Testing
**Non-negotiable.** Any reputable NMN brand should provide third-party lab results showing:
- Purity (should be 99%+)
- Heavy metals testing
- Microbial testing
- Identity verification

### 2. Proper Storage & Shipping
NMN is sensitive to heat and moisture. Look for:
- Cold storage or temperature-controlled shipping
- Dark/opaque packaging (light degrades NMN)
- Desiccant packets in containers

### 3. Transparent Sourcing
The brand should clearly state:
- Where their NMN is manufactured
- The production process (enzymatic synthesis preferred)
- Quality control procedures

## Where NOT to Buy

### ❌ Amazon (Usually)
While convenient, Amazon NMN has problems:
- Third-party sellers may sell fake or degraded products
- Storage conditions unknown (hot warehouses)
- Counterfeit products are common
- No guarantee of proper cold chain

### ❌ Unknown Chinese Manufacturers
- Quality control varies wildly
- May contain fillers or contaminants
- No third-party verification
- If it seems too cheap, it probably is

### ❌ eBay / Random Websites
- No quality guarantees
- Counterfeit risk is high
- No recourse if product is bad

## Red Flags to Watch For

🚩 **No third-party testing** — If they can't show lab results, walk away
🚩 **Too cheap** — Quality NMN costs $0.50-1.00/gram. If it's $0.10/gram, it's probably fake
🚩 **No purity claims** — Reputable brands proudly display 99%+ purity
🚩 **Made in unknown facilities** — GMP-certified facilities are essential
🚩 **No reviews from real users** — Check Reddit, not just the brand's website
🚩 **Exaggerated claims** — "Cures aging!" or "Reverses time!" are red flags

## Buying Tips

1. **Start with powder** — Cheapest way to try NMN
2. **Buy from brand websites** — Not third-party sellers
3. **Check expiration dates** — NMN degrades over time
4. **Store properly** — Refrigerate after opening
5. **Buy in bulk** — Many brands offer discounts for 3-6 month supplies
