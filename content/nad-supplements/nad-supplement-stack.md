NAD+ precursors work best when combined with synergistic supplements. Here's how to build the ultimate longevity stack.

## The Core Stack

### 1. NAD+ Precursor (NMN or NR)
**The foundation of your stack**

Choose one:
- NMN: 500-1000mg (morning)
- NR: 300-600mg (morning)

### 2. TMG (Trimethylglycine)
**Methylation support — essential companion**

Why: NAD+ precursors consume methyl groups during metabolism. TMG replenishes them, preventing potential side effects.

- Dose: 500mg
- Timing: With your NAD+ precursor

### 3. Resveratrol
**Sirtuin activator — works synergistically with NAD+**

Why: Resveratrol activates sirtuins (NAD+-dependent enzymes). More NAD+ + more sirtuin activation = enhanced longevity signaling.

- Dose: 250-500mg
- Timing: With breakfast (fat-soluble, take with food)
- Form: Trans-resveratrol (more bioavailable)

## Advanced Additions

### 4. Quercetin
**Senolytic + NMN enhancer**

Why: Quercetin helps clear senescent ("zombie") cells and may enhance NMN absorption.

- Dose: 500mg
- Timing: With breakfast

### 5. CoQ10 (Ubiquinol)
**Mitochondrial support**

Why: Supports the same mitochondrial pathways as NAD+. Ubiquinol is the active, more bioavailable form.

- Dose: 100-200mg
- Timing: With breakfast (fat-soluble)

### 6. Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)
**Anti-inflammatory foundation**

Why: Reduces chronic inflammation, supports brain health, and works synergistically with NAD+ pathways.

- Dose: 2-3g combined EPA/DHA
- Timing: With meals

### 7. Vitamin D3 + K2
**Essential for overall health**

Why: Supports immune function, bone health, and cellular signaling. Many people are deficient.

- Dose: 2000-5000 IU D3 + 100mcg K2
- Timing: With breakfast

## What to Avoid in Your Stack

### ❌ NAD+ itself (oral)
Oral NAD+ has poor bioavailability. Your stomach acid breaks it down before absorption. Stick to precursors (NMN/NR).

### ❌ Too many antioxidants
Megadosing antioxidants can actually blunt the beneficial stress response that NAD+ precursors trigger. Moderate doses are better.

### ❌ Cheap, untested products
Quality matters enormously. Third-party tested products from reputable brands are worth the extra cost.

## Monitoring Your Progress

Track these biomarkers to assess your stack's effectiveness:

- **NAD+ blood levels** — Available through specialized labs
- **hsCRP** — Inflammation marker
- **Fasting insulin/glucose** — Metabolic health
- **DHEA-S** — Hormone marker
- **Telomere length** — Long-term aging marker (expensive)
