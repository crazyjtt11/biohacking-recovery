NAD+, NMN, and NR are related but different compounds. Here's a clear breakdown of each.

## The Relationship

Think of it as a conversion chain:

**NR → NMN → NAD+**

- **NR (Nicotinamide Riboside):** Two steps away from NAD+
- **NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide):** One step away from NAD+
- **NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide):** The active molecule your cells use

## Head-to-Head Comparison

| Factor | NAD+ | NMN | NR |
|--------|------|-----|-----|
| What it is | Active coenzyme | NAD+ precursor | NAD+ precursor |
| Conversion steps | Already active | 1 step | 2 steps |
| Oral bioavailability | Poor | Good | Good |
| Typical dose | 50-200mg | 500-1000mg | 300-600mg |
| Price range | $20-50/month | $30-80/month | $25-60/month |
| Human studies | Limited | Growing (20+) | Extensive (30+) |
| FDA status | N/A | Not GRAS | GRAS |
| Stability | Low | Moderate | High |
| Best form | IV/sublingual | Sublingual/capsule | Capsule |

## Which Should You Take?

### Take NAD+ if:
- You're getting IV NAD+ therapy (best bioavailability)
- You want direct NAD+ supplementation (sublingual)
- You're under medical supervision

### Take NMN if:
- You want the most direct oral precursor
- You're interested in the latest research
- You prefer sublingual delivery

### Take NR if:
- You want the most clinically proven option
- FDA approval matters to you
- You prefer a well-established supplement

## The Practical Answer

**For most people, NMN or NR is the better choice** because:
- Oral NAD+ has poor bioavailability (stomach acid destroys it)
- NMN and NR are efficiently absorbed
- They're converted to NAD+ inside your cells

**NMN has a slight edge** because it's one conversion step closer to NAD+.

**NR has a slight edge** because it has more human clinical data and FDA approval.

## Can You Take All Three?

Some biohackers combine NMN and NR. While this is safe, there's no evidence it's better than taking one at an adequate dose. The body will convert whichever is available to NAD+.

## The Bottom Line

- **Best direct precursor:** NMN (one step to NAD+)
- **Most evidence:** NR (30+ human trials)
- **Best delivery:** IV NAD+ (but expensive and inconvenient)
- **Best value:** NMN or NR powder

For most people, 500mg NMN or 300mg NR daily is the sweet spot.
