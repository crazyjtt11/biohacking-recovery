NAD+ is essential for energy production during exercise. Can supplementing with NAD+ precursors improve athletic performance?

## NAD+'s Role in Exercise

### 1. Energy Production
During exercise, your muscles need massive amounts of ATP. NAD+ is critical for:
- Glycolysis (fast energy)
- Aerobic metabolism (sustained energy)
- Fat oxidation (endurance energy)

### 2. Mitochondrial Function
Mitochondria are the powerhouses of muscle cells. NAD+:
- Supports mitochondrial biogenesis (creating new mitochondria)
- Enhances mitochondrial efficiency
- Improves oxygen utilization

### 3. Recovery
NAD+ supports post-exercise recovery by:
- Reducing inflammation
- Supporting DNA repair
- Enhancing cellular repair processes

## Research Findings

### NMN and Exercise

**2021 (Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition):**
- 600mg NMN for 6 weeks
- Improved aerobic capacity in amateur runners
- Enhanced oxygen utilization

**2023 (Frontiers in Nutrition):**
- 1200mg NMN for 6 weeks
- Improved time to exhaustion
- Enhanced endurance performance

### NR and Exercise

**2020 (Cell Reports Medicine):**
- NR supplementation in older adults
- Improved muscle NAD+ levels
- Enhanced exercise capacity

## Stacking for Performance

**Pre-Workout Stack:**
- NMN 500mg (energy)
- Caffeine 100-200mg (focus)
- Beta-alanine 3g (endurance)

**Post-Workout Stack:**
- NMN 500mg (recovery)
- Protein 25-30g (muscle repair)
- Creatine 5g (strength)

## The Bottom Line

NAD+ supplementation shows promise for improving exercise performance, particularly:
- Endurance capacity
- Oxygen utilization
- Recovery speed
- Mitochondrial function

However, it's not a magic pill. Combine with proper training, nutrition, and rest.
