NAD+ plays a critical role in liver function. Here's what the research shows.

## NAD+'s Role in Liver Health

### 1. Metabolism
The liver is the body's metabolic hub. NAD+ supports:
- Fat metabolism
- Glucose regulation
- Detoxification processes

### 2. Fat Processing
NAD+ helps prevent fatty liver:
- Enhances fat oxidation
- Reduces fat accumulation
- Supports healthy liver fat levels

### 3. Detoxification
NAD+-dependent enzymes support:
- Phase I and Phase II detoxification
- Alcohol metabolism
- Drug processing

## Research Findings

### NR and Liver Health
**2020 (Hepatology):**
- NR reduced liver fat in NAFLD patients
- Decreased liver inflammation
- Improved liver enzymes

### NMN and Liver
**2019 (Cell Metabolism):**
- NMN protected against liver damage in mice
- Enhanced liver regeneration
- Reduced fibrosis markers

## Practical Implications

### For Liver Support
- NMN 500mg or NR 300mg daily
- Combine with milk thistle and NAC
- Limit alcohol consumption
- Maintain healthy weight
