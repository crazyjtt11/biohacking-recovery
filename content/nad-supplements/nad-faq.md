Common questions about NAD+ supplements answered.

## What is NAD+?
A coenzyme essential for energy production, DNA repair, and cellular function. Levels decline with age.

## NMN or NR?
Both are effective. NMN is one step closer to NAD+. NR has more clinical data. Either works well.

## How much should I take?
- NMN: 500-1000mg/day
- NR: 300-600mg/day

## When should I take it?
Morning. NAD+ precursors can be energizing and may disrupt sleep if taken late.

## Are there side effects?
Rare and mild: nausea, headaches. Add TMG to prevent headaches.

## Is it safe?
Yes. Both NMN and NR have excellent safety profiles in human clinical trials.

## How long until results?
- Energy: 1-2 weeks
- Anti-aging: 2-3 months
- Consistent use is key
