When you take your NAD+ supplements can significantly affect their effectiveness. Here's the optimal timing guide.

## Best Time: Morning

### Why Morning is Best

1. **Aligns with circadian rhythm** — NAD+ levels naturally peak in the morning
2. **Energizing effect** — NAD+ precursors can boost energy (avoid evening dosing)
3. **Sirtuin activity** — Sirtuins (NAD+-dependent enzymes) are most active in the morning
4. **Metabolic support** — Morning dosing supports daytime metabolism
5. **Sleep protection** — Avoids potential sleep disruption from energizing effects

### What Happens If You Take It at Night?

Some people report:
- Difficulty falling asleep
- More vivid dreams
- Restlessness
- Increased alertness at bedtime

Not everyone is affected, but morning dosing eliminates this risk.

## With Food or Empty Stomach?

### NMN

**Both work, but:**
- **Empty stomach** — May improve absorption slightly
- **Sublingual (under tongue)** — Best absorption, bypasses gut entirely
- **With food** — Reduces nausea risk for sensitive individuals

**Recommendation:** Try empty stomach first. If you get nausea, take with food.

### NR

**Both work equally well:**
- NR is well-absorbed with or without food
- No significant difference in bioavailability
- Take whichever is more convenient

**Recommendation:** Take with breakfast for consistency.

## Timing by Goal

| Goal | Best Timing | Reason |
|------|------------|--------|
| General wellness | Morning | Aligns with natural rhythm |
| Anti-aging | Morning | Maximum sirtuin activation |
| Energy boost | Morning | Energizing effect |
| Athletic performance | Morning + pre-workout | Performance support |
| Sleep optimization | Morning only | Avoids sleep disruption |

## Combining with Other Supplements

**Take WITH your NAD+ precursor:**
- TMG (same time, supports methylation)

**Take WITH BREAKFAST (can be same meal):**
- Resveratrol (fat-soluble, needs food)
- CoQ10 (fat-soluble, needs food)
- Vitamin D3 (fat-soluble, needs food)
- Omega-3 (fat-soluble, needs food)

**Take SEPARATELY:**
- Quercetin (may compete with NMN absorption, take 30 min apart)

## Common Timing Mistakes

### ❌ Taking at night
Most common mistake. The energizing effect can disrupt sleep.

### ❌ Inconsistent timing
Taking it at random times reduces effectiveness. Consistent morning dosing is best.

### ❌ Taking with coffee immediately
Coffee's acidity may affect some supplements. Wait 15-30 minutes after coffee, or take supplements before coffee.

### ❌ Skipping days
NAD+ supplementation works best with consistent daily use. Skipping days reduces cumulative benefits.
