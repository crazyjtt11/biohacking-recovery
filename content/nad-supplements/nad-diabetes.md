NAD+ plays a role in glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity. Here's what the research shows.

## NAD+'s Role in Blood Sugar

### 1. Insulin Sensitivity
NAD+-dependent enzymes (sirtuins) regulate insulin signaling:
- SIRT1 enhances insulin sensitivity
- Improves glucose uptake in cells
- Supports healthy blood sugar levels

### 2. Mitochondrial Function
Proper mitochondrial function is essential for:
- Glucose metabolism
- Energy production from sugar
- Preventing insulin resistance

### 3. Pancreatic Function
NAD+ supports beta cell function:
- Insulin production
- Beta cell survival
- Glucose sensing

## Research Findings

### NMN and Insulin Sensitivity
**2021 (Science):**
- NMN improved muscle insulin sensitivity in prediabetic women
- Enhanced glucose metabolism
- Suggested potential for diabetes prevention

### NR and Blood Sugar
**2020 (Nature Communications):**
- NR improved metabolic markers in overweight adults
- Reduced fasting glucose
- Enhanced insulin sensitivity

## Practical Implications

### For Blood Sugar Support
- NMN 500-1000mg or NR 300-600mg daily
- Morning supplementation
- Combine with proper diet and exercise
- Monitor blood sugar regularly

### Important Warnings
- NAD+ supplements are NOT a replacement for diabetes medication
- Consult your endocrinologist before starting
- Monitor blood sugar closely when starting supplements
- May interact with diabetes medications
