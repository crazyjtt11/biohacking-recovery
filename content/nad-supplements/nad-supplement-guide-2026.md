## What is NAD+?

NAD+ is a coenzyme found in every cell, essential for:
- Energy production
- DNA repair
- Cellular signaling
- Hundreds of metabolic reactions

NAD+ levels decline ~50% between ages 40-60, contributing to aging and age-related diseases.

## NAD+ Precursors

### NMN (Nicotinamide Mononucleotide)
- One step from NAD+
- Growing human research
- Popular in biohacking community
- **Dose:** 500-1000mg/day

### NR (Nicotinamide Riboside)
- Two steps from NAD+
- Extensive human research
- FDA GRAS status
- **Dose:** 300-600mg/day
