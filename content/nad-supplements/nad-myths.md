There's a lot of hype around NAD+ supplements. Let's separate fact from fiction.

## Myth 1: "NAD+ Supplements Reverse Aging"

**Overstated.** NAD+ supplements may slow some aspects of aging by supporting cellular repair, but they don't reverse aging. The research is promising but not conclusive.

## Myth 2: "You Can Feel NAD+ Working Immediately"

**False.** Most benefits take weeks to months to appear. Some people feel more energy quickly, but the anti-aging effects are gradual.

## Myth 3: "More NAD+ is Always Better"

**False.** There's an optimal range. Excessive NAD+ supplementation hasn't been shown to provide additional benefits and may have unknown long-term effects.

## Myth 4: "NMN is Infinitely Better Than NR"

**False.** Both are effective NAD+ precursors. NMN is one step closer to NAD+, but NR has more clinical data. The difference is marginal.

## Myth 5: "NAD+ Supplements are Completely Safe with No Risks"

**Mostly true, but with caveats.** While NMN/NR have excellent safety profiles, long-term human data is still accumulating. Always consult your doctor.

## Myth 6: "NAD+ Supplements Replace a Healthy Lifestyle"

**False.** Supplements complement, not replace, proper diet, exercise, sleep, and stress management.

## Myth 7: "All NAD+ Supplements Are the Same"

**False.** Quality varies enormously. Third-party testing, purity, and proper storage all matter significantly.

## The Bottom Line

NAD+ supplements are promising but not magic. Approach with realistic expectations:
- They may support cellular health
- Results take time
- Quality matters
- Lifestyle still matters most
