NAD+ plays a critical role in cardiovascular health. Here's what the research shows.

## NAD+'s Role in Heart Health

### 1. Energy Production
The heart is the most energy-demanding organ. NAD+ is essential for:
- Cardiac muscle energy production
- Heart contractile function
- Maintaining cardiac output

### 2. Blood Vessel Health
NAD+-dependent enzymes support:
- Endothelial function (vessel lining)
- Nitric oxide production (vasodilation)
- Blood vessel flexibility

### 3. Blood Pressure Regulation
NAD+ helps regulate blood pressure through:
- Vasodilation support
- Inflammation reduction
- Vascular tone management

## Research Findings

### NR and Blood Pressure
**2019 (Scientific Reports):**
- 1000mg NR for 8 weeks
- Reduced systolic blood pressure by 5mmHg
- Improved arterial stiffness

### NMN and Vascular Health
**2021 (Cell Reports):**
- NMN improved blood vessel function in aged mice
- Enhanced capillary density
- Improved blood flow

## Practical Implications

### For Heart Health Support
- NMN 500-1000mg or NR 300-600mg daily
- Morning supplementation
- Combine with omega-3 and CoQ10
- Regular cardiovascular exercise

### Important Notes
- NAD+ supplements are not a replacement for heart medication
- Consult your cardiologist before starting
- Monitor blood pressure regularly
