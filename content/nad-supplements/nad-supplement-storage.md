Proper storage is crucial for maintaining NMN/NR supplement potency. Here's how to store them correctly.

## Why Storage Matters

NMN and NR are sensitive to:
- **Heat** — Degrades the molecules
- **Moisture** — Causes breakdown
- **Light** — UV light degrades NAD+ precursors
- **Oxygen** — Oxidation reduces potency

## Storage Guidelines

### NMN

| Factor | Recommendation |
|--------|----------------|
| Temperature | Cool (below 77°F/25°C) |
| Ideal | Refrigerator (35-46°F/2-8°C) |
| Light | Dark storage (opaque container) |
| Moisture | Desiccant packet included |
| Container | Original sealed container |
| Shelf life | 12-24 months (sealed), 6-12 months (opened) |

### NR

| Factor | Recommendation |
|--------|----------------|
| Temperature | Room temperature OK |
| Ideal | Cool, dark place |
| Light | Protected from light |
| Moisture | Keep container sealed |
| Container | Original container |
| Shelf life | 24+ months (NR is more stable) |

## Storage Tips

### Do ✅
- Store in the refrigerator (NMN)
- Keep container sealed when not in use
- Include desiccant packet
- Use original container
- Check expiration dates

### Don't ❌
- Leave in hot car or direct sunlight
- Store in bathroom (humidity)
- Transfer to different containers
- Use expired supplements
- Freeze (may damage capsules)

## Signs of Degradation

If your NMN/NR has degraded:
- Change in color (yellowing)
- Unusual smell
- Clumping or caking
- Different texture

If you notice these signs, replace the product.
