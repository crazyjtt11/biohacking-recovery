## Our Mission

BioHacking Recovery is your trusted source for evidence-based information on recovery science, biohacking tools, and optimization strategies.

We combine peer-reviewed research with hands-on testing to help you make informed decisions about cold plunge tubs, red light therapy devices, NAD+ supplements, and other recovery tools.

## What We Cover

- **Cold Plunge Therapy** — Reviews, science, and buying guides for cold plunge tubs and ice baths
- **Red Light Therapy** — Device comparisons, treatment protocols, and clinical research
- **NAD+ Supplements** — Product reviews, dosage guides, and the latest research on NMN, NR, and NAD+
- **Biohacking Recovery** — Saunas, PEMF, compression therapy, and emerging recovery technologies

## Our Review Process

Every product we recommend goes through a rigorous evaluation:

1. **Research** — We analyze clinical studies, manufacturer claims, and user feedback
2. **Testing** — Where possible, we test products hands-on
3. **Comparison** — We compare across price points, features, and value
4. **Transparency** — We disclose affiliate relationships and never accept payment for positive reviews
