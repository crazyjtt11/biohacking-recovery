## What is Red Light Therapy?

Red light therapy (RLT) uses specific wavelengths of light to stimulate cellular healing:
- **Red light (630-660nm):** Skin and superficial tissue
- **Near-infrared (810-850nm):** Deep tissue, joints, brain

## How It Works

1. Photons penetrate the skin
2. Absorbed by mitochondria (cytochrome c oxidase)
3. ATP (cellular energy) production increases
4. Cellular repair accelerates
5. Inflammation decreases
