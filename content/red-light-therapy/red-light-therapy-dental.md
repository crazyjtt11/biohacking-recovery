Red light therapy has surprising benefits for oral health. Here's what the research shows.

## Oral Health Benefits

### 1. Gum Disease Treatment
RLT can help with periodontal disease by:
- Reducing gum inflammation
- Killing harmful bacteria
- Promoting tissue repair
- Reducing pocket depth

### 2. Wound Healing After Dental Procedures
After extractions, implants, or surgery:
- Faster healing
- Reduced pain
- Less swelling
- Better tissue regeneration

### 3. TMJ/TMD Relief
For temporomandibular joint disorders:
- Reduces jaw pain
- Decreases inflammation
- Improves jaw function

### 4. Tooth Sensitivity
RLT may help with sensitive teeth by:
- Reducing nerve inflammation
- Promoting dentin repair
- Calming irritated nerves

## Clinical Evidence

**2019 (Journal of Photochemistry and Photobiology):**
- LLLT reduced gum inflammation in periodontitis patients
- Improved healing after dental surgery
- Reduced pain scores

**2017 (Lasers in Medical Science):**
- RLT accelerated healing after tooth extraction
- Reduced post-operative pain
- Less swelling and bruising
