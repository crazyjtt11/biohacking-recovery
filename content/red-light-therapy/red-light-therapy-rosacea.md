Rosacea causes persistent facial redness. Can red light therapy help?

## How RLT May Help Rosacea

### 1. Reduces Inflammation
Rosacea involves chronic facial inflammation:
- RLT calms inflammatory response
- Reduces redness
- Decreases flare frequency

### 2. Strengthens Skin Barrier
Rosacea often involves a compromised skin barrier:
- RLT supports skin barrier repair
- Improves moisture retention
- Reduces sensitivity

### 3. Blood Vessel Support
Rosacea involves visible blood vessels:
- RLT may reduce vessel visibility
- Improves vessel health
- Calms vascular inflammation

## Treatment Protocol

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Wavelength | 660nm (red light) |
| Duration | 5-10 minutes |
| Frequency | Daily |
| Distance | 6-12 inches |
| Timeline | 8-12 weeks for improvement |

## Important Notes

- RLT is not a cure for rosacea
- Continue prescribed treatments
- Consult your dermatologist
- Individual responses vary
