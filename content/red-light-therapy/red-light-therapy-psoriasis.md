Psoriasis affects 8 million Americans. Red light therapy may help manage symptoms.

## How RLT May Help Psoriasis

### 1. Slows Cell Turnover
Psoriasis involves rapid skin cell growth. RLT:
- May slow abnormal cell proliferation
- Reduces plaque formation
- Normalizes skin cell cycle

### 2. Reduces Inflammation
RLT calms the overactive immune response:
- Decreases inflammatory cytokines
- Reduces redness and swelling
- Calms irritated skin

### 3. Promotes Healing
RLT supports tissue repair:
- Stimulates healthy skin cell growth
- Reduces scarring
- Improves skin texture

## Clinical Evidence

### Phototherapy History
Psoriasis has been treated with light therapy for decades:
- UV phototherapy is established treatment
- RLT offers similar benefits without UV risks
- Some studies show improvement with red/NIR light

## Treatment Protocol

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Wavelength | 660nm + 850nm |
| Duration | 10-15 minutes per area |
| Frequency | Daily |
| Timeline | 8-12 weeks for improvement |

## Important Notes

- RLT is not a cure for psoriasis
- Continue prescribed treatments
- Consult your dermatologist
- Individual responses vary
