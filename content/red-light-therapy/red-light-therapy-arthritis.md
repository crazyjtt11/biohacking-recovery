Arthritis affects 58 million Americans. Red light therapy offers a non-invasive, drug-free approach to managing arthritis pain.

## Types of Arthritis Treated

### Osteoarthritis (OA)
- Most common form (wear and tear)
- Affects joints, cartilage, and surrounding tissue
- Strong evidence for RLT treatment

### Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA)
- Autoimmune condition
- Causes chronic inflammation in joints
- Moderate evidence for RLT treatment

## How RLT Helps Arthritis

1. **Reduces joint inflammation** — Decreases inflammatory markers in joint fluid
2. **Stimulates cartilage repair** — Promotes chondrocyte (cartilage cell) activity
3. **Reduces pain signals** — Modulates nerve conduction in joints
4. **Improves blood flow** — Enhances circulation to joint tissue
5. **Decreases morning stiffness** — Common complaint in RA patients

## Clinical Evidence

### Osteoarthritis
**2019 Meta-Analysis (Lasers in Medical Science):**
- 22 RCTs analyzed
- Significant pain reduction in knee OA
- Improved physical function
- Best results with 810-830nm wavelength

### Rheumatoid Arthritis
**2020 Study (Photomedicine and Laser Surgery):**
- 40% reduction in morning stiffness
- Decreased pain scores
- Improved grip strength
- Well-tolerated with no side effects

## Treatment Protocol

### For Knee Osteoarthritis
| Parameter | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Wavelength | 810-830nm (near-infrared) |
| Duration | 10-15 minutes per knee |
| Frequency | 3-5x/week |
| Distance | Direct contact to 6 inches |
| Timeline | 6-12 weeks for significant improvement |

### For Hand Arthritis
| Parameter | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Wavelength | 660nm + 850nm |
| Duration | 5-10 minutes per hand |
| Frequency | Daily |
| Distance | Direct contact |
| Timeline | 4-8 weeks for improvement |
