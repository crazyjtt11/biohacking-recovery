Red light therapy is a growing business opportunity. Here's how to get started.

## Market Opportunity

### Industry Size
- Red light therapy market valued at $1+ billion
- Growing 15%+ annually
- Increasing consumer awareness
- Expanding applications (skin, pain, recovery)

### Target Customers
- Skincare enthusiasts
- Chronic pain sufferers
- Athletes
- Anti-aging seekers
- Wellness-conscious consumers

## Business Models

### 1. RLT Studio
- Multiple red light therapy devices
- Walk-in appointments
- Membership options
- **Startup cost:** $30,000-100,000

### 2. RLT + Wellness Center
- Combine with other modalities
- Sauna, cryotherapy, massage
- Premium positioning
- **Startup cost:** $100,000-500,000

### 3. Mobile RLT Services
- Bring RLT to clients
- Corporate wellness
- Events and pop-ups
- **Startup cost:** $10,000-30,000

## Revenue Model

### Pricing
- Single session: $25-75
- Monthly membership: $100-300
- Package deals: $200-500
- Premium treatments: $100-200
