Stretch marks affect up to 80% of people. Can red light therapy help reduce their appearance?

## How RLT May Help Stretch Marks

### 1. Collagen Remodeling
Stretch marks are caused by collagen breakdown. RLT:
- Stimulates new collagen production
- Helps remodel scar tissue
- Improves skin structure

### 2. Increased Blood Flow
Better circulation to stretch marks:
- Delivers nutrients for repair
- Removes damaged tissue
- Promotes healing

### 3. Reduced Inflammation
Chronic inflammation in stretch mark tissue:
- RLT calms inflammation
- Promotes healthier tissue
- May prevent worsening

## Clinical Evidence

### Limited But Promising
- Some studies show improvement in scar appearance
- Collagen stimulation is well-proven
- More research needed for stretch marks specifically

## Treatment Protocol

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Wavelength | 660nm + 850nm |
| Duration | 10-15 minutes per area |
| Frequency | Daily |
| Timeline | 12-24 weeks for visible improvement |
| Expectations | May reduce appearance, unlikely to eliminate completely |
