Autoimmune conditions affect 24 million Americans. Can red light therapy help manage symptoms?

## How RLT May Help Autoimmune Conditions

### 1. Immune Modulation
RLT doesn't just suppress or stimulate the immune system — it modulates it:
- Calms overactive immune response
- Reduces inflammatory cytokines
- Promotes regulatory T cells

### 2. Inflammation Reduction
Chronic inflammation is the hallmark of autoimmune disease:
- RLT reduces inflammatory markers
- Calms tissue inflammation
- May reduce flare frequency

### 3. Pain Relief
Autoimmune conditions often cause chronic pain:
- RLT reduces pain signals
- Improves joint function
- Decreases reliance on pain medication

## Conditions That May Benefit

### Rheumatoid Arthritis
- Strong evidence for joint pain relief
- Reduced inflammation
- Improved function

### Psoriasis
- May slow cell turnover
- Reduces plaque formation
- Calms inflammation

### Lupus
- Limited research
- May help with skin symptoms
- Consult rheumatologist

### Hashimoto's
- May reduce thyroid inflammation
- Limited research
- Consult endocrinologist

## Important Cautions

- **Consult your doctor** before starting RLT for autoimmune conditions
- **Don't stop prescribed medications** without medical guidance
- **Monitor symptoms** carefully
- **Individual responses vary** significantly
