Red light therapy is one of the most effective non-invasive anti-aging treatments. Here's the complete guide.

## How RLT Fights Aging

### 1. Collagen Stimulation
Collagen production declines ~1% per year after age 25. RLT:
- Stimulates fibroblast activity
- Increases collagen synthesis by up to 200%
- Improves skin structure and firmness

### 2. Elastin Production
Elastin gives skin its bounce-back quality. RLT:
- Supports elastin production
- Improves skin elasticity
- Reduces sagging

### 3. Cellular Energy
Aging cells have less energy (ATP). RLT:
- Boosts mitochondrial function
- Increases ATP production
- Enhances cellular repair

### 4. Inflammation Reduction
Chronic inflammation accelerates aging. RLT:
- Reduces inflammatory markers
- Calms irritated skin
- Prevents premature aging

## Clinical Evidence
