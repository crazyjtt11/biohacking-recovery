Hair loss affects millions of people. Red light therapy (LLLT) is FDA-cleared for treating hair loss, but does it actually work?

## How Red Light Therapy Helps Hair

Red light therapy stimulates hair growth through several mechanisms:

1. **Stimulates hair follicle stem cells** — Wakes up dormant follicles
2. **Increases blood flow to scalp** — Delivers more nutrients to hair roots
3. **Reduces inflammation** — Chronic inflammation contributes to hair loss
4. **Extends anagen (growth) phase** — Keeps hair in the growing phase longer
5. **Increases ATP production** — Gives follicle cells more energy to grow

## Clinical Evidence

### FDA Clearance
In 2007, the FDA cleared LLLT (low-level laser therapy) for treating androgenetic alopecia (pattern hair loss) in both men and women.

### Key Studies

**2014 (Lasers in Surgery and Medicine):**
- 100 participants with androgenetic alopecia
- Red light therapy for 25 weeks, 3x/week
- Results: 39% increase in hair count

**2013 (International Journal of Trichology):**
- 41 men with pattern baldness
- LLLT for 24 weeks
- Results: Significant improvement in hair density and thickness

**2018 (Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology):**
- Meta-analysis of 11 studies
- Conclusion: LLLT is effective for androgenetic alopecia
- Both men and women showed improvement

|-----------|----------------|
| Wavelength | 650-670nm (red light) |
| Duration | 10-25 minutes |
| Frequency | 3-5x/week |
| Timeline | 12-24 weeks for visible results |
| Maintenance | 2-3x/week ongoing |

## Realistic Expectations

### What to expect:
- **Month 1-3:** Possible increase in shedding (normal, old hairs making way for new)
- **Month 3-6:** New hair growth becomes visible
- **Month 6-12:** Significant improvement in density and thickness
- **Ongoing:** Maintenance sessions to keep results

### What RLT can't do:
- Regrow hair on completely bald areas (no follicles left)
- Work instantly — patience is required
- Replace hair transplant for advanced hair loss
- Stop hair loss from progressing (it slows it, doesn't stop it)

## Combining with Other Treatments

Red light therapy works best when combined with:

- **Minoxidil (Rogaine)** — Enhances absorption, complementary mechanism
- **Finasteride (Propecia)** — Addresses hormonal component
- **Biotin supplements** — Supports hair health
- **Scalp massage** — Improves blood flow
- **Healthy diet** — Provides building blocks for hair growth
