Chronic pain affects 50 million Americans. Red light therapy offers a drug-free, non-invasive approach to pain management with growing clinical support.

## How Red Light Therapy Reduces Pain

### Mechanisms of Pain Relief

1. **Reduces inflammation** — Decreases pro-inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6)
2. **Blocks pain signals** — Modulates nerve fiber activity and conduction velocity
3. **Increases endorphins** — Stimulates natural pain-killing chemicals
4. **Improves circulation** — Brings oxygen and nutrients to damaged tissue
5. **Accelerates tissue repair** — Speeds healing of damaged nerves, muscles, and joints

## Pain Conditions Treated

### Back Pain

**Evidence:** Multiple RCTs show significant improvement in chronic low back pain with LLLT.

**Protocol:**
- Wavelength: 830nm (near-infrared)
- Duration: 15-20 minutes
- Frequency: Daily for 2-4 weeks, then 3x/week maintenance
- Application: Direct to affected area

### Neck Pain

**Evidence:** A 2019 meta-analysis found LLLT significantly reduced neck pain compared to placebo.

**Protocol:**
- Wavelength: 830nm
- Duration: 10-15 minutes
- Frequency: Daily for 3-4 weeks
- Application: Direct to cervical spine area

### Knee Osteoarthritis

**Evidence:** Strong evidence from multiple meta-analyses. A 2019 review of 22 RCTs confirmed significant pain reduction.

**Protocol:**
- Wavelength: 810-830nm
- Duration: 10-15 minutes per knee
- Frequency: 3-5x/week for 6-12 weeks
- Application: Around the entire knee joint

### Neuropathy (Nerve Pain)

**Evidence:** Growing evidence for peripheral neuropathy, including diabetic neuropathy.

**Protocol:**
- Wavelength: 830nm
- Duration: 10-15 minutes
- Frequency: Daily
- Application: Along affected nerve pathway

### Tendinitis

**Evidence:** Good evidence for Achilles tendinitis, rotator cuff tendinitis, and lateral epicondylitis (tennis elbow).

**Protocol:**
- Wavelength: 820-830nm
- Duration: 10-15 minutes
- Frequency: Daily for 4-6 weeks
- Application: Direct to tendon
