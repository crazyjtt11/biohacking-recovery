NR Supplement Guide: Nicotinamide Riboside Benefits & Dosage

Complete guide to NR (Nicotinamide Riboside) supplements. How it works, benefits, dosage, and best products.

📅 April 30, 2026 📖 446 words · 2 min read
#NR #nicotinamide riboside #supplements #NAD

NR (Nicotinamide Riboside) was the first NAD+ precursor to gain mainstream attention. Here’s your complete guide to NR supplementation.

What is NR?

NR (Nicotinamide Riboside) is a form of vitamin B3 that your body converts to NAD+. It was discovered in 2004 by Dr. Charles Brenner and has been extensively studied since.

The conversion pathway: NR → NMN → NAD+

NR is two steps away from NAD+, compared to NMN’s one step. However, NR has more human clinical data than any other NAD+ precursor.

How NR Works

  1. NR enters cells — Easily absorbed through the gut
  2. NRK enzyme converts NR to NMN — Using the enzyme nicotinamide riboside kinase
  3. NMN is converted to NAD+ — Through the NMNAT enzyme
  4. NAD+ levels rise — Supporting cellular energy and repair

Clinical Evidence

NR has the strongest clinical evidence of any NAD+ precursor:

Key Human Studies

2016 (Nature Communications):

  • First human NR study
  • 1000mg/day for 7 days
  • NAD+ levels increased by 60%
  • Safe and well-tolerated

2017 (American Journal of Clinical Nutrition):

  • 2000mg/day for 12 weeks
  • Significant NAD+ increase
  • No adverse effects

2018 (Scientific Reports):

  • 1000mg/day for 8 weeks
  • Improved blood pressure in pre-hypertensive adults
  • Reduced aortic stiffness

Multiple Tru Niagen studies:

  • 30+ human clinical trials
  • Consistent safety and efficacy data
  • FDA GRAS status

Benefits of NR

Well-Established

  • Raises NAD+ levels — Consistently shown in human studies
  • Safe and well-tolerated — No serious adverse effects in any trial
  • Improves blood pressure — Multiple studies show modest BP reduction
  • Supports metabolic health — May improve insulin sensitivity

Promising

  • Neuroprotection — May support brain health
  • Cardiovascular support — Improved arterial function
  • Exercise performance — Some studies show improved muscle recovery
  • Liver health — May support liver function

Side Effects

NR has an excellent safety profile:

  • No serious adverse effects in any clinical trial
  • Mild nausea possible at high doses
  • May cause flushing (similar to niacin)
  • Generally very well-tolerated
