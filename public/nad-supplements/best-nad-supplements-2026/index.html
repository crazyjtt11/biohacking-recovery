NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is one of the most important molecules in your body. It’s essential for energy production, DNA repair, and cellular health. NAD+ levels decline with age, which is why NAD+ supplementation has become a cornerstone of the biohacking community.

What is NAD+?#

NAD+ is a coenzyme found in every living cell. It plays critical roles in:

  • Energy metabolism — Converts food into cellular energy (ATP)
  • DNA repair — Activates sirtuins and PARPs that repair damaged DNA
  • Cellular signaling — Regulates hundreds of metabolic processes
  • Circadian rhythm — Helps maintain healthy sleep-wake cycles

NAD+ levels drop by approximately 50% between ages 40 and 60, contributing to many age-related health declines.

Frequently Asked Questions#

When will I notice effects?#

Many users report increased energy within 1-2 weeks. More significant anti-aging benefits take 2-3 months of consistent use.

Are there side effects?#

NAD+ precursors are generally well-tolerated. Some users report mild nausea, flushing, or headaches at high doses. Start low and increase gradually.

NMN or NR — which is better?#

Current research suggests NMN may be more effective at raising NAD+ levels, but NR has more human clinical trials. Both are good options. NMN is generally preferred by the biohacking community.

Should I take NAD+ supplements with food?#

NMN and NR can be taken with or without food. Some research suggests taking them on an empty stomach may improve absorption.
